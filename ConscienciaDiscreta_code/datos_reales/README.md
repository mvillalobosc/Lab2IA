## Español

Datos humanos públicos de dos experimentos de fusión de dos destellos. Cada subcarpeta tiene su propio README con el detalle de sus archivos.

| Carpeta | Fuente | Qué trae |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [deodato_melcher_2024](deodato_melcher_2024/) | OSF, <https://doi.org/10.17605/osf.io/acb9n> | Curvas psicométricas de 50 participantes (38 incluidos por los autores), con destellos de 10 ms y siete ISI de 10 a 70 ms |
| [haarlem_2024](haarlem_2024/) | Zenodo, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10844750> (CC BY 4.0) | Umbral de fusión y frecuencia alfa individual en reposo y prestímulo de 31 participantes, y el curso temporal grupal de la frecuencia alfa prestímulo |

Los datos conservan los términos de sus autores. Si los usa, cite las fuentes originales:

- Deodato, M., & Melcher, D. (2024). Aperiodic EEG predicts variability of visual temporal processing. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 44(40), e2308232024. <https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2308-23.2024>
- Deodato, M., & Melcher, D. (2024). Correlations between visual temporal resolution and individual alpha peak frequency: evidence that internal and measurement noise drive null findings. *Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience*, 36(4), 590-601. <https://doi.org/10.1162/jocn_a_01993>
- Deodato, M. (2023). *Correlations between visual temporal resolution and individual alpha peak frequency* [conjunto de datos]. OSF. <https://doi.org/10.17605/osf.io/acb9n>
- Haarlem, C. S., Mitchell, K. J., Jackson, A. L., & O'Connell, R. G. (2024). Individual peak alpha frequency correlates with visual temporal resolution, but only under specific task conditions. *European Journal of Neuroscience*, 60(7), 5591-5604. <https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.16519>
- Haarlem, C. S. (2024). *Alpha-band oscillations and visual temporal resolution: an expansion and partial replication of Samaha & Postle's 2015 study* [conjunto de datos, versión v1]. Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10844750>

## English

Public human data from two two-flash fusion experiments. Each subfolder has its own README with the detail of its files.

| Folder | Source | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [deodato_melcher_2024](deodato_melcher_2024/) | OSF, <https://doi.org/10.17605/osf.io/acb9n> | Psychometric curves of 50 participants (38 included by the authors), with 10 ms flashes and seven ISIs from 10 to 70 ms |
| [haarlem_2024](haarlem_2024/) | Zenodo, <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10844750> (CC BY 4.0) | Fusion threshold and resting and pre-stimulus individual alpha frequency of 31 participants, and the group time course of the pre-stimulus alpha frequency |

The data keep the terms of their authors. If you use them, please cite the original sources listed above.
