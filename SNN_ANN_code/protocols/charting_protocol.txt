ELIGIBILITY CONFIRMATION AND DATA CHARTING PROTOCOL (full abstract)
Systematic review: energy efficiency of ANN vs SNN and sparsity / lottery-ticket methods (2020-2026)

Each record below passed title/abstract screening (some were marked UNSURE). You now see the FULL abstract. For EACH record: (1) confirm eligibility; (2) if eligible, code the data items. Judge only from the text given (title, keywords, abstract). Never guess a value the text does not support: use NS (not stated) instead.

RESEARCH QUESTIONS
RQ1. Under which model-hardware pairs and measurement protocols have ANN and SNN been compared empirically?
RQ2. Which factors (hardware substrate, numerical precision, time steps, spike coding, firing activity) explain reported accuracy and efficiency differences?
RQ3. What do pruning, sparsity and lottery-ticket-family methods contribute to reducing cost in ANN and SNN, and how is their effect on energy or firing activity measured?

ELIGIBILITY. A record is eligible (Y) if at least one category applies:
C1  Empirical ANN-SNN comparison on at least one task with at least one efficiency metric (energy, power, latency, throughput, SOPs/FLOPs/MACs, energy-delay product), measured on hardware OR estimated analytically. Includes ANN-to-SNN conversion papers and hardware/accelerator papers that benchmark an SNN against an ANN/DNN baseline with such metrics. A quantified claim such as "N x less energy than the ANN counterpart" counts, and so does an explicit statement such as "fewer operations than its ANN counterpart". NOT C1: an accuracy-only comparison; an SNN whose only efficiency figure is its own number of time steps; an SNN compared only with other SNNs or with CPU/GPU runs of the same SNN.
C2  Pruning or sparsification in SNN: weight, connection, neuron, channel or time-step pruning; regularisation or mechanisms that reduce spiking activity; dynamic sparsity and sparse training. Quantisation-only or knowledge-distillation-only work is NOT C2.
C3  Lottery-ticket family in non-spiking ANN (LTH, strong LTH, supermasks, edge-popup, random-weight subnetworks, winning tickets, existence proofs, mask search by any algorithm).
C4  Lottery-ticket family applied to SNN.
C5  Review, survey or tutorial whose main subject is ANN-SNN efficiency comparison, neuromorphic hardware benchmarking, or pruning/sparsity/LTH.
C6  Benchmark or measurement methodology for ANN/SNN efficiency (benchmark suites, energy estimation frameworks, metric standardisation).
If NOT eligible (N), give exactly one reason:
E1 not a research or review paper; E2 device/material/circuit level without network-level evaluation against an ANN or involving pruning; E3 neuroscience modelling without an ML task; E4 SNN-only work without an ANN efficiency comparison and without pruning/sparsity/LTH; E5 ANN-only work without SNN and without LTH-family content; E6 off-topic; E7 general ANN pruning/sparsification outside the lottery-ticket family and without SNN.
