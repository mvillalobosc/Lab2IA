FULL-TEXT RETRIEVAL, ELIGIBILITY AND DATA EXTRACTION PROTOCOL
Systematic review: energy efficiency of ANN vs SNN and lottery-ticket/sparsity methods (2020-2026)

You receive a short list of candidate studies (id, title, year, authors, venue, DOI, abstract-level codes and note). For EACH study: (1) retrieve a legal open full text; (2) assess eligibility for the in-depth synthesis; (3) extract data. Never invent a value: every number you write must appear in the text you read. When a value is not reported, write NR.

1. RETRIEVAL (stop at the first success; at most 4 web tool calls per study, then declare NOT_RETRIEVED)
 a. If a DOI is given, WebFetch https://doi.org/<DOI> with the extraction prompt below (open-access publishers such as MDPI, Frontiers, Nature, PLOS, IEEE Access, open Springer or Elsevier articles usually work).
 b. Otherwise, or if (a) fails or shows only an abstract/paywall: WebSearch the exact title (in quotes) plus arXiv. If an arXiv record of the SAME work exists, WebFetch https://arxiv.org/html/<id> (or https://arxiv.org/abs/<id> and then https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>) with the extraction prompt.
 c. Open repositories found in search results (PMC, Europe PMC, university repositories, the authors' pages, OpenReview, CVF open access, PMLR, NeurIPS proceedings) are acceptable.
 Never use Sci-Hub, LibGen or any unauthorised mirror. If a site is declined, move on.
 Record retrieval = PUBLISHER_OA | ARXIV | REPOSITORY | NOT_RETRIEVED and the URL actually used. Record version = PUBLISHED or PREPRINT.
 Use ONE comprehensive WebFetch prompt per document asking for all items of sections 2 and 3 with short verbatim quotes and the table, figure or section where each number appears. Use the offset parameter to continue a long page only if the needed tables are not in the first part.

2. ELIGIBILITY FOR THE IN-DEPTH SYNTHESIS (judged on the full text)
 INCLUDED (paired measured comparison) when ALL hold:
  P1 the authors themselves evaluate at least one ANN and at least one SNN on the same task or dataset;
  P2 accuracy or the task metric is reported for both;
  P3 the energy, power or energy per inference of the SNN is MEASURED on physical hardware (neuromorphic chip, FPGA, ASIC, MCU, or CPU/GPU with power read from a meter, on-board sensor or vendor tool during the run);
  P4 the efficiency of the ANN is measured on an identified physical platform in the same study.
 Otherwise EXCLUDED with one reason:
  FT1 no ANN evaluated by the authors (comparison only with other SNNs or with figures from other papers);
  FT2 efficiency only estimated analytically (operation counts times energy per operation, simulators, synthesis reports) for the SNN, the ANN or both;
  FT3 SNN measured on hardware but the ANN efficiency is not measured or its platform is not identified;
  FT4 no accuracy or task metric for both paradigms;
  FT5 not an empirical comparison (review, position paper, demonstration without results).
 Studies whose abstract-level categories include C4 (lottery tickets in SNN) get decision INCLUDE_C4 if the full text confirms a lottery-ticket-family method applied to an SNN, regardless of P3 and P4.

3. EXTRACTION
 3a. For INCLUDED studies write one line per ANN-SNN pair (at most 3 pairs per study; choose the main result first). 26 tab-separated fields:
  id | pair | task_dataset | ann_model | ann_platform | ann_precision | snn_model | snn_platform | snn_precision | neuron_model | snn_training (CONVERSION|SURROGATE|STDP|OTHER|NR) | timesteps_T | coding (RATE|TEMPORAL|DIRECT|DELTA|POPULATION|EVENT_SENSOR|OTHER|NR) | acc_ann | acc_snn | energy_ann (value and unit per inference) | energy_snn | latency_ann | latency_snn | energy_type_ann (DYNAMIC|TOTAL|NR) | energy_type_snn (DYNAMIC|TOTAL|NR) | measurement (how power or energy was obtained, at most 12 words) | batch_size | firing_activity (spike rate, spikes per inference or SOPs if reported) | quotes (at most 3 short verbatim quotes with table/figure/section, separated by " || ") | notes (at most 20 words)
 3b. For INCLUDE_C4 studies write one line with 12 tab-separated fields:
  id | method | lth_variant (WEAK|STRONG|BOTH|OTHER) | search_algorithm (IMP|SCORE|EVO|OTHER) | datasets | sparsity_reached | acc_dense | acc_ticket | cost_reported (what energy, SOPs, firing or hardware figures are reported, or NONE) | hardware | quotes (at most 3, separated by " || ") | notes
 3c. For EVERY study write one assessment line with 8 tab-separated fields:
  id | retrieval | url | version | decision (INCLUDED|INCLUDE_C4|EXCLUDED|NOT_RETRIEVED) | reason (FT1..FT5 or -) | precision_note (numeric precision of each platform if stated, else NR) | justification (at most 25 words)

4. OUTPUT
 Write three files with the Write tool (no header lines, tab-separated, no tabs inside fields):
  <batch>_eval.tsv  (one line per study, all studies)
  <batch>_pares.tsv (pairs of INCLUDED studies; write an empty file if none)
  <batch>_c4.tsv    (INCLUDE_C4 studies; write an empty file if none)
 Write the files after finishing all studies of the batch. Reply only with the counts per decision and the ids NOT_RETRIEVED.
